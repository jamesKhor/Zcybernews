## Executive Summary

A May 2026 webinar hosted by Radiant Security, promoted via The Hacker News, highlights a persistent operational gap in security operations centers (SOCs): high-risk alerts from web application firewalls (WAF), data loss prevention (DLP), operational technology/Internet of Things (OT/IoT) environments, dark web intelligence feeds, and supply chain signals are frequently deprioritized or ignored entirely. The session, titled "What the Riskiest SOC Alerts Go Unanswered — and How Radiant Security Can Help," frames the problem as one of blind spots rather than sheer alert volume, according to the promotional material. No specific incident, vulnerability, threat actor, or technical indicator is disclosed; the content is a vendor-led discussion of operational best practices.

## Technical Analysis

The webinar's thesis, as described in the announcement, is that SOC teams are not simply overwhelmed by the number of alerts but are systematically missing the most consequential ones. The five alert categories singled out — WAF, DLP, OT/IoT, dark web intelligence, and supply chain signals — represent distinct detection domains that often fall outside traditional SIEM correlation rules or require specialized context to triage effectively. For example, WAF alerts may be dismissed as false positives from legitimate traffic patterns, while OT/IoT alerts demand knowledge of industrial protocols that generalist analysts lack. Dark web intelligence feeds produce unstructured data that is difficult to operationalize at speed. The promotional copy does not provide quantitative data (e.g., mean time to respond, false-positive rates, or specific case studies) to substantiate the claim, and no independent research is cited. The webinar is positioned as a solution-oriented discussion rather than a disclosure of new findings.

## Mitigations & Recommendations

Defenders should audit their SOC's alert triage workflows to identify categories that are systematically ignored or delayed. For each of the five highlighted domains — WAF, DLP, OT/IoT, dark web, supply chain — SOC managers should verify that dedicated playbooks exist, that analysts have the necessary training to interpret domain-specific signals, and that automated enrichment (e.g., threat intelligence lookups, asset criticality scoring) is applied before alerts reach human reviewers. Organizations that lack in-house expertise for OT/IoT or dark web analysis should consider outsourcing tier-1 triage for those feeds or implementing machine-learning-based prioritization engines. The webinar itself is a vendor resource; readers should evaluate Radiant Security's platform claims against their own operational requirements.
